LUJI'S is an Argentinian food truck in Miami serving handcrafted empanadas, made fresh every day, right on our truck.

Every empanada is made from scratch and folded by hand, honoring Argentina's culinary tradition. We use only grass-fed beef and farm-fresh produce in every bite, finished with our homemade chimichurri.

From the truck window to private events, corporate catering and wholesale, we bring sabor argentino wherever people gather.
